## Description: <br>
Create and edit Obsidian Bases (.base files) with views, filters, formulas, and summaries. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[psprosen-dev](https://clawhub.ai/user/psprosen-dev) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, writers, and Obsidian users use this skill to create and revise `.base` files that organize notes into table, card, list, or map views with filters, formulas, summaries, and YAML validation guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated `.base` filters, formulas, or vault paths could include notes or metadata the user did not intend to surface. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated filters, formulas, and target vault paths before applying changes, then validate the YAML and rendered view in Obsidian. <br>
